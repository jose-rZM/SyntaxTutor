\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Outcome of parsing user-\/written grammar text. 

When {\ttfamily \doxylink{struct_grammar_parse_result_a46793251259c570f93c197d7800cd388}{errors}} is empty, {\ttfamily \doxylink{struct_grammar_parse_result_ad218a7c8345d3d8e967af09d8072788b}{grammar}} holds a fully-\/built grammar: symbols are classified (non-\/terminals are exactly the left-\/hand sides), the axiom is the left-\/hand side of the first rule, and the grammar has been augmented with a fresh axiom rule {\ttfamily X -\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} \texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}axiom\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} \$} following the app-\/wide convention.

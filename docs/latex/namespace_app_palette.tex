void App\+Palette::\+apply (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}{}{}\end{DoxyParamCaption})\hspace{0.3cm}{\ttfamily [inline]}}



Palette entries the theme needs that app.\+qss cannot set. 

Links in rich text (QLabel, QText\+Browser) take their colour from the palette; a style sheet rule on {\ttfamily a} has no effect. The platform\textquotesingle{}s light palette makes them dark blue, barely readable on this dark theme -\/ which is what Linux showed, while mac\+OS hid it with a dark palette of its own.
